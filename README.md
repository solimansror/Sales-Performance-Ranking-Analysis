# Sales Performance & Ranking Dashboard 📊🚀

## 📌 Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales performance across multiple business dimensions.

The dashboard combines revenue analysis, order tracking, channel contribution, product performance, and salesperson ranking to provide a complete view of business performance.

---

## 🚨 Business Problem

Businesses need clear visibility into sales performance to make better strategic decisions.

However, raw transactional data alone does not easily reveal:

- Which sales channels generate the highest revenue
- Which products drive business growth
- Which sales representatives perform best
- How sales efficiency changes over time

This project addresses those challenges through interactive analytics and KPI monitoring.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze sales revenue and order performance
- Measure Average Ticket Price (ATP)
- Compare channel contribution
- Evaluate product category performance
- Rank sales representatives
- Support sales optimization and business planning

---

## 🛠 Tools Used

- Power BI
- DAX
- Excel
- Data Modeling

---

## 📊 Key KPIs

| KPI | Value |
|------|------|
| Revenue | Total Sales Revenue |
| Orders | Total Completed Orders |
| ATP | Revenue ÷ Orders |
| Channel Revenue | Retail / Distributor / Online |
| Product Categories | Food / Drink |
| Ranking Metrics | Revenue, Orders, ATP |

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### Sales Dashboard

- Revenue trend analysis
- Orders monitoring
- ATP calculation
- Revenue by channel
- Revenue by product group
- Supervisor performance analysis
- Product category insights

### Ranking Dashboard

- Top performing sales representatives
- Revenue ranking analysis
- Orders comparison
- ATP performance tracking
- Contribution percentage evaluation

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Retail is the strongest revenue channel.
- Food products contribute the largest share of category orders.
- Top salespeople generate a significant portion of overall revenue.
- ATP provides visibility into average transaction efficiency.
- Ranking analysis highlights performance differences across the sales team.

---

## 💡 Business Value

This dashboard helps businesses:

- Monitor sales performance
- Improve sales strategy
- Identify high-value channels
- Optimize team performance
- Support data-driven decision making
